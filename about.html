In the water

I swam for NYU Men’s Swimming & Diving (NCAA Division III) from 2023 to 2025. I swam the butterfly leg of our conference-winning 400 Medley Relay, which set a program record and earned First Team honors.

I was also in the team’s leadership group, where I organized team-building and mental health programs and brought in speakers for the wider athletics department.

That’s why this site is full of water.

On land

I run marathons and follow fitness tech. I like Miyazaki films, coffee and tea, and finding new places to eat.

I also follow China–US policy and macroeconomics.
