Context

A national NGO came to EmpowerED Consulting, a student pro bono consulting group, for a 12-week engagement on its assessment product. The client’s name and the original materials are left out.

Problem

Leadership had to decide whether to build an assessment product or buy one, and if buying, which of many options fit both the mission and the budget.

What I did

I was Project Manager, leading 5 consultants alongside a co-PM. We screened 20 assessment products against mission and cost criteria down to 3 finalists, then wrote a product requirements document with assessment question frameworks and a risk report to ground the recommendation.

I revised ~60 deliverables into client-ready decks, presented at 5 executive client meetings and owned all client communication.

Result

We recommended a $5M acquisition and a 12-month integration roadmap. The client signed a follow-on engagement, and the next phase of the work was built on our deliverables.
