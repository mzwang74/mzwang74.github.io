The four engagements

I oversaw 4 pro bono engagements with New York organizations and stayed in close contact with each client, so the work they got was work they’d actually use:

Education Through Music, the largest nonprofit provider of in-school music education in NYC; Future Meets Present, a climate-focused social design organization behind the Marketplace of the Future event; Project New Yorker; and W!SE.

2 of the 4, Project New Yorker and W!SE, are returning as clients in Spring 2027.

How I ran it

Standardized how projects start. Every team built a 12-week engagement overview before kickoff: about 3 pages with project phases, weekly milestones and a Gantt chart, adapted to each client’s needs.

Managed people directly. I held weekly 1:1s with every PM, ran midterm reviews for all 17 consultants, and checked in with clients myself to make sure they were satisfied.

Professional development

I co-hosted about 5 workshops on networking, industry exploration, and case and behavioral interviews, with 20–30 members at each. I brought in outside speakers and supported first-time PD chairs as they found their footing.
